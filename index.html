Frequently Asked Questions

Everything About Hiring a Fractional CTO

What is a Fractional CTO and how does it work?

A Fractional CTO is an experienced executive software architect who provides part-time or project-based technical leadership to early-stage startups. Instead of paying high annual salaries, founders get strategic technical direction, architecture design, hands-on code execution, and team management at a fraction of the cost.

When should an early-stage startup hire a Fractional CTO?

You need a Fractional CTO when: (1) Non-technical founders are building an MVP and need end-to-end technical execution, (2) You need high-level architecture decisions for AI, web, or mobile apps without hiring full-time, (3) You want to avoid technical debt before pitching investors or raising seed funding.

How is a Fractional CTO different from hiring freelancers or agencies?

Freelancers execute single tasks without understanding your business model. Agencies often assign junior developers and charge high overhead markups. A Fractional CTO works directly with you as an accountable technical partner—handling full-stack development, database architecture, AI integration, and growth engineering under one unified strategy.

What technical stack and capabilities do you cover?

I cover 360° product engineering: Full-stack web applications, native & cross-platform Android/iOS mobile apps, scalable Node.js/Python backends, PostgreSQL/MongoDB databases, AI chatbots (LLMs/RAG), automated WhatsApp/SMS messaging, e-commerce (Shopify/WooCommerce), and technical SEO.
